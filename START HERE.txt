RECLAIMED SPACE CRAFTERS - ONE-CLICK SET UP

1. Double-click  "Install RSC.command"  in this folder.
   The first time, macOS may say it can't verify the file. If so, right-click (or
   Control-click) it, choose Open, then Open again. That is the only extra step.

2. Wait. The first install downloads the AI models (a few GB), so let it run.
   It asks nothing and needs no administrator password.

3. When it finishes, the app opens in your browser on the Setup page. Type the
   business Gmail address, paste its app password (the page links to where to make
   one), and the email addresses that should get the Monday report. Press Save.

After that, double-click RSC on the Desktop any time. The app also starts by itself
when you log in, checks your email every 30 minutes, and sends the weekly report
Monday mornings.

Everything runs on this Mac. Your jobs and receipts stay here.
If anything stops, send Myles a picture of the Terminal window.
