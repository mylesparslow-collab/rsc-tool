Bulkhead replacement and outdoor projects in Saratoga Springs, NY

A rusted bulkhead or a cellar stair you don’t trust is the kind of small job many contractors pass on. We take it, along with outdoor showers, garden bridges and other exterior carpentry.

Get a quote Call (518) 932-9919

What we build

  • Bulkhead door and hatch replacement
  • New bulkhead treads
  • Concrete cellar steps
  • Outdoor showers
  • Garden bridges and structures

Bulkheads take a beating. Leaves pile up on them, water sits on the steps, and every winter the freeze-thaw works at the edges. A new door, hatch or set of treads keeps the cellar entrance dry and safe to use.

We also build custom exterior projects, such as a wood outdoor shower or a footbridge over a backyard pond.

Signs a bulkhead needs replacing

Rust or rot at the edges, a hatch that won’t close tight, water on the cellar steps after rain, or steps that flex underfoot.

Doors, hatches and steps

The work can range from replacing the treads to installing a new door, hatch and concrete steps. Your estimate specifies exactly what is included.

Custom outdoor projects

For outdoor showers, garden bridges and other custom structures, describe what you have in mind and we will tell you what is feasible.

Designed for winter

Exterior work in Saratoga County has to withstand snow, ice and freeze-thaw cycles, so we plan where the water goes and use materials and fasteners that hold up outside.

Frequently asked questions

What people usually ask before we come out.

Can you replace an old bulkhead door?

Yes. Bulkhead doors, hatches and treads can be replaced as a job of their own, and the cellar steps below can be rebuilt in concrete.

Will you take on a small or unusual outdoor project?

Yes. We build outdoor showers, garden bridges and other custom structures.
