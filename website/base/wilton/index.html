Common projects in Wilton

  • Replacing original builder-grade decks
  • Stair and railing rebuilds
  • Updating original kitchens
  • Bathroom remodels and refreshes
  • Built-ins, trim and storage

Much of Wilton’s housing was built in the last few decades, and many of those homes are reaching the age where the original deck, kitchen and bathrooms need replacement.

The underlying structure is usually sound. That allows us to rebuild a deck on a solid frame, update a kitchen without major demolition, and modernize a bathroom, either as separate projects or together.

Original decks

Pressure-treated decks built in the 1990s and 2000s are reaching the end of their service life. Soft boards, loose railings and unstable stairs are typically the first signs. A composite deck on a new or reinforced frame is a common replacement.

Built for upstate winters

Decks and stairs in Saratoga County have to carry snow load and withstand freeze-thaw cycles, so footings, framing and fasteners matter as much as the decking.

Updating the existing layout

Keeping the existing kitchen or bathroom layout costs considerably less than relocating plumbing and walls. We will advise you on which changes are worth the cost.

Permits

The Town of Wilton issues its own building permits, which are required for new decks and most remodels. We plan for permits and inspections from the outset.

Frequently asked questions

What homeowners usually ask before a site visit.

Do you work in Wilton?

Yes. Wilton borders Saratoga Springs and is part of our regular service area, along with Gansevoort, Greenfield and Moreau.

Should I repair or replace an original deck?

If the posts, beams and joists are sound, new decking, stairs and rails can extend its life by years. If the framing is deteriorated or pulling away from the house, a full rebuild is the safer choice. We assess the structure at the site visit.

How does pricing work?

After a site visit, we provide a written estimate with the scope of work, the price and a payment schedule. If the scope changes during the job, we agree on the change and its cost in writing before proceeding.
