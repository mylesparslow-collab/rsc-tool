What we build in the city

  • Porch rails, balusters and steps on older houses
  • Kitchens designed around original brick and trim
  • Bathroom remodels and refreshes
  • Decks, stairs and railings
  • Built-ins, staircase repair and restoration carpentry

Many Saratoga Springs homes are well built and full of original detail, with plaster walls, turned newels, deep porches and century-old brick.

We try to keep that detail and still make the house work for the way people live now. Recent projects include a kitchen built around its original brick chimney, new scroll-cut porch balusters, and a staircase restored rather than replaced.

Historic districts

Parts of the city sit in historic districts, where exterior changes can need Design Review Commission approval before work starts. If your home is in one, we include that review in the project plan and schedule.

Planning around track season

The city is busiest in July and August, and many homeowners rent their houses during the meet. If you plan to rent, we schedule exterior work to finish before the season begins.

Unforeseen conditions

Opening an older wall can reveal rot, knob-and-tube wiring or unexpected framing. When that happens, we tell you before going further, and any change to the scope or price is agreed in writing before work continues.

Permits

Decks, structural changes and most remodels require a city building permit. We plan for permits and inspections from the start.

Frequently asked questions

What people usually ask before we come out.

Are you based in Saratoga Springs?

Yes. Refined Space Crafters is a design-build firm based in Saratoga Springs, and most of our work is within a short drive of the city.

Can you work on an older home without losing its original detail?

Yes. Where practical, we repair original trim, brick, stairs and porches or rebuild them to match, and we finish new work to suit the house.

My house is in a historic district. Can you still do exterior work?

Yes. Exterior changes in the city’s historic districts may require Design Review Commission approval, and we build that step into the plan and schedule.

How does pricing work?

We come out and look first. Then you get a written estimate with the scope of work, the price and a payment schedule. If something changes during the job, we agree on the change and the cost in writing before we keep going.
