Restoration and built-ins in Saratoga Springs, NY

Keep the turned newel, the porch balusters and the old staircase. We repair what can be saved, rebuild what can’t to match, and build new pieces that look like they were always there.

Get a quote Call (518) 932-9919

What we build

  • Staircases: treads, risers, newels and rails
  • Cellar stair rebuilds
  • Porch balusters and rails
  • Ceiling repairs and tin-look ceilings
  • Benches, chests and bookcases
  • Wood-framed glass partitions and French doors

Older houses often need smaller work than an addition. We fix stairs, rebuild porch rails and build benches into corners that have never been good for anything.

Many older homes in the Saratoga area have details worth keeping, such as turned newels, scroll-cut porch balusters and solid stairs under layers of paint. We repair what can be saved and build replacements to match.

Repair before replace

A worn stair, loose newel or failing porch rail can often be repaired, refinished or reinforced rather than replaced, which preserves the original material.

Built-ins that fit the room

We build benches, chests, bookcases and partitions to fit their location, aligned with the surrounding walls, windows and trim.

Ceilings and plaster

Cracked plaster ceilings can often be repaired or covered rather than removed. The tin-look ceiling on this page was installed over the original cracked ceiling.

Matching new work to old

Where a part cannot be saved, we make the replacement to match what remains, as with the new newel and rail at the landing of the restored staircase.

Frequently asked questions

What people usually ask before we come out.

What kind of restoration work do you take on?

Staircases, cellar stairs, porch balusters and railings, ceilings and interior trim. If something in an older home needs repair, ask us.

Do you build custom built-ins?

Yes. We build benches, chests, bookcases, desks and wood-framed glass partitions to fit the room.

Do you work in my town?

We work across Saratoga County and the Capital Region, including Saratoga Springs, Ballston Spa, Malta, Wilton, Greenfield, Milton, Gansevoort, Stillwater, Mechanicville, Schuylerville, Corinth, Clifton Park, Halfmoon, Round Lake, Glens Falls and Queensbury. If your town isn’t listed, ask us.
