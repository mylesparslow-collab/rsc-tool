For new homeowners

New house? Let’s walk through it together.

Most new owners have a list: the bathroom you would do differently, the kitchen that almost works, the steps that give a little underfoot. We’ll walk the house with you at no charge, tell you plainly what needs doing now and what can wait, and give you a written plan before any work starts.

Book a walkthroughCall (518) 932-9919

1. You show us your list

Kitchen, bathrooms, stairs, porch, trim: whatever is on your mind, plus anything you’d like a second set of eyes on.

2. You get plain answers

What is sound, what can wait, and what it would take to do the work properly. No pressure and no sales pitch.

3. You get a written plan

If you want to go ahead, you get a written scope and price before anything starts. If not, you keep the advice.

4. New work that fits the house

Our crew designs and builds it, with trim, stairs and porches matched to what is already there.
