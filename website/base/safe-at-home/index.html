Safe at Home

Home safety assessments and modifications for older homeowners and their families in Saratoga County. This service is not yet available; join the waiting list to be notified when it opens.

Join the listCall (518) 932-9919

What Safe at Home will cover

  • Home safety assessment
  • Handrails on both sides of stairways
  • Grab bars anchored into framing
  • Safer entry steps and landings
  • Better lighting in stairways, halls and entries
  • Accessible bathroom modifications

Most people want to remain in their own homes as they age. Often a few targeted changes to stairs, entries and bathrooms make that possible.

We already build stairs, railings, entry steps and bathrooms. Safe at Home looks at what would make your house safer and easier to live in as you get older. Each project begins with an assessment and a written list of recommendations in order of priority.

1. Home assessment

We review the home with you, inside and out, including entry steps, stairways, bathrooms, bedrooms and the routes between them.

2. Written recommendations

You receive a prioritized list of recommended changes with prices, so you can address the most important items first and plan the rest over time.

3. Targeted improvements

Handrails, grab bars, lighting and steps are typically small projects. Their safety depends on proper anchoring and construction.

4. Larger modifications

Projects such as walk-in showers or first-floor living space are planned with you and your family when they are needed.

Funding assistance

Some homeowners qualify for programs that cover safety and accessibility modifications. Availability changes, so contact the programs directly to confirm current eligibility.

Rebuilding Together Saratoga County

A Ballston Spa nonprofit that repairs and modifies homes for older, disabled and income-eligible homeowners in Saratoga County. Call (518) 587-3315.

NYS Access to Home

A New York State program that helps pay for accessibility changes for people with disabilities, run through local nonprofits. Rebuilding Together can tell you whether it is taking applications.
