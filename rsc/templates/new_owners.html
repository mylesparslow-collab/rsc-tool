New homeowners

People who just bought a house in your towns, from the county's public property records. New owners often redo a kitchen or bathroom in their first year or two. Each house gets up to three cards by mail: a welcome, a follow-up three weeks later, and a seasonal note six weeks after that. Every card has a QR code and a short code for {{ short_link }}, where they can ask for a free walkthrough. Nothing is sent for you, and there are no calls or texts.

The cards

Words in curly brackets are filled in for each house: {addressee}, {street}, {town}, {owner}, {business}, {region}, {phone}, {email}. The seasonal card also has {season_note}, a line about the season it goes out in. The QR code, {{ short_link }} and the house's code are printed under every card on their own. Keep it about the quality of the work and a friendly welcome. No prices or deadlines.
