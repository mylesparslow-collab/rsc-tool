How a job goes, start to finish

Reclaimed Space Crafters

Open the app by double-clicking RSC on the Desktop. Gray boxes like this are the buttons you press. Nothing goes to a client until you press a button.

  1. A new customer writes in The app checks your email every 10 minutes. A new inquiry shows up under Inbox › To review with a reply already written that offers your next visit times.
  2. Answer them Read the reply, change anything you want, and press Open reply in Mail. Send it from Mail as usual.
  3. Make the job On the same card press Start estimate. Or go to Jobs › All jobs › New job and paste their email or type a few lines. The job gets a number like 1003-DEL.
  4. The estimate On Estimate & contract, type your notes and press Draft the estimate. Read Please check these at the top: those are guesses. To fix anything, type it in the change box, like "labor is $11,000", and press Make the change.
  5. Check the price A colored box says Above your floor or Below your floor. It also warns you if the work comes in under $65 a crew-hour. Red means raise the price.
  6. Contract and signature Press Print the estimate (SOW) and View the contract (draft), print them, and have the client sign on paper. Then press Contract signed.
  7. Payment schedule "Contract signed" makes the payments, like 1003-DEL-1, -2, -3. Use that number as the QuickBooks invoice number. When money arrives, press Mark paid in To review.
  8. Start the work On the job page set Status to In Progress and press Save. Now the job shows on the Today tab, and the crew gets that sheet by email at 6 AM on weekdays. Tick steps off as they're done.
  9. Hours, expenses, receipts Hours: type "Tue Mike 8, Dave 6 on Delano" and press Log hours. Expenses: type "Home Depot 109.91 for Delano" and press Read it, or take a picture of the receipt and press Read the photo. Check it, then press Add expense.
  10. The client changes something On the job press Change orders, type what changed with the price, and press Write it up. The client signs on the screen with Sign change order, and the total and payments update.
  11. Keep the client posted On the job press Client update, add photos, press Write this week's update, then Open in Mail. For late payments, Money › Money owed › Write a reminder.
  12. Monday report Every Monday at 7 AM you and Myles get an email with the money and any jobs losing money. Those jobs also show on Home as "watch the margin."
  13. Finish the job Walk it with the client, set Status to Complete, and press Save. Under Inbox › Follow-ups a review request and a one-year check-in wait for you: Write the message, send it, then Mark sent.
Always check the prices. The assistant's numbers are guesses unless you gave them.
Talk instead of typing: tap the round microphone on any big box.
Not sure what's next? Home lists the next step for each job.
Stuck? Close the black window and double-click RSC again.
