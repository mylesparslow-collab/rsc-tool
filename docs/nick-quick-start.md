# Nick's quick start

**Open it:** double-click **RSC** on the Desktop. Your browser opens the app. Leave the black window open while you use it.

**Every day:** open **To Review**. The assistant has read your email and suggests expenses, new leads and client payments. Fix anything wrong, then press the green button. Nothing is added until you do.

**New job:** Jobs > New job. Paste the client's email or type a few lines (who, where, what they want, any prices you already know). The assistant makes the job and drafts the whole estimate: phases, steps and prices. Anything it had to guess is listed at the top. To change something, just type it in the "Want something different?" box, like "labor is $11,000". Then create the payment schedule and print the estimate and contract.

**Expenses:** type one line, like "Home Depot 109.91 studs for Smith", and press Read it. Check what it filled in and press Add.

**Receipt photos:** on Expenses, press the photo box, take a picture of the receipt (or pick one that was texted to you), add a few words like "for Smith", and press Read the photo. Check it and press Add; the photo is saved with the expense. You can also email the photo to the business address and it shows up in To Review.

**QuickBooks:** open the QuickBooks tab and download the invoices, expenses or customers file, then import it in QuickBooks. The invoice number is the Payment ID.

**New customers:** an inquiry in To Review comes with a reply already written, offering your next visit times. Read it, change anything, press Open reply in Mail.

**After a job:** when you mark a job Complete, the Follow-ups tab queues a review request and a one-year check-in. Open one, press Write the message, send it from Mail, press Mark sent.

**Monday report:** every Monday morning you and Myles get an email with the week's money and jobs to watch. See it any time under Home > Weekly report.

**Changed scope:** open the job > Change orders. Type what changed with the price, like "second window on the east wall, $1,200". The client signs on the screen and the contract total and payments update.

**Talk instead of typing:** the big text boxes have a round microphone button. Tap it, say what you want, tap it again. The words appear in the box. The first time, the browser asks to use the microphone: press Allow.

**Always check the prices.** The assistant's prices are rough guesses unless you gave the number. Nothing is billed until you press "Contract signed".

**IDs:** job 1002-SMI, payment 1002-SMI-3 (use it as the QuickBooks invoice number), change order 1002-SMI-CO1.

**Stuck?** Quit the black window and double-click RSC again. Your data is in the RSC folder in your home folder.
